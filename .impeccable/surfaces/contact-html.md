# BMA conversation and quote page
Mode: Operate. Established BMA world. User requests local booking destination with real embedded calendar, supplied pointing John, and an email quote option.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Visitors choose a real appointment without leaving BMA, with a direct mail alternative for quote requests.
OWN-WORLD: Existing BMA typography, pale blue surface, dark blue controls, generous white calendar area. Supplied transparent John points into the calendar from its left.
STORY: Understand the 15-minute introduction, select a date and time in Cal.com, or compose an email request to Lio. Do not invent availability or booking confirmation.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Standard header, compact introduction on the left and a prominent email quote panel on the right. Below, the calendar occupies the right column and the supplied John stands against its left edge in the reserved left column. Mobile stacks introduction, email option and calendar, with an 88px John beside the calendar heading. Both layouts show the email address and a labeled mail button.
FORM: User-pinned two-column booking flow inside the existing brand, with standard external calendar controls. seed: none (unavailable).
